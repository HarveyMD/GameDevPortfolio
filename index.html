About Me
I'm a Games and Software Programmer based in Mansfield, specialising in Unreal Engine and Unity with strong proficiency in C++, C# and Python.

I graduated with a BSc in Computer Games Programming from the University of Derby in 2023, and have since completed two industry-focused Skills Bootcamps — most recently an Interactive Real-Time 3D (iRT3D) Bootcamp with Infinity27 in 2025 — keeping my practical skills sharp and up to date.

My main strengths are UI/UX programming and gameplay systems, backed up by hands-on experience in low-level graphics (building 3D engines in C++ and DirectX from scratch) and team-based development across multiple game jams and university projects.

I'm currently open to opportunities in game development and broader software roles, and actively working on personal projects to stay engaged with the latest tools and techniques.
