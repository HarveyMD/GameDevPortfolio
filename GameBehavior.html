Game Behavior
A solo third-year university project built in Unreal Engine, originally aimed at exploring physics interactions and AI behaviors. While I did experiment with these systems, my main focus ended up being on level design and building out a responsive weapon system.

The project included a custom-designed level and a working weapon mechanic, with basic AI and physics systems implemented in the background to support gameplay. This experience helped me get more hands-on with Unreal Engine's Blueprint system and reinforced key gameplay programming principles.
